HarmonyHands

Open your hand and your own voice grows a live backing-vocal stack underneath it. Close it (or drop it out of frame) and you hear just your natural voice. There's nothing to set up first — no key, no part, no notes to pick. It listens to what you're singing, figures out the key on its own, and follows you.

Hand detection uses MediaPipe Hands and voice harmonization uses Tone.js's real-time pitch shifter — both run entirely in your browser; no audio or video leaves your device. Gesture logic: an open hand (≥3 fingers extended) brings in 4 backing-harmony voices; a closed fist, an ambiguous pose, or no hand in frame plays just your natural voice. There's no key or part to set — a running pitch listener passively figures out the most likely key you're singing in (the same way a backing singer picks it up by ear after a few bars), and each harmony voice tracks a fixed scale-step away from whatever note you're on (a 3rd up, a 5th up, a 3rd down, a 6th down), snapped to that key. The lead voice itself gets a light, partial nudge toward the nearest in-key pitch — a touch of pitch correction, blended under the untouched dry signal rather than fully quantizing it. Use "Test the 4 harmony voices" after starting to hear the current harmony shape on its own.
