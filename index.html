Hand detection uses MediaPipe Hands and voice harmonization uses Tone.js's real-time pitch shifter — both run entirely in your browser; no audio or video leaves your device. Gesture logic: an open hand (≥3 fingers extended) splits your live voice into the full five-part harmony; a closed fist, an ambiguous pose, or no hand in frame plays just your natural voice. The four harmony voices are fixed intervals above/below whatever note you're singing (stacked thirds and fifths outlining a major chord on your note) — no key to set, it always tracks your live pitch directly. Use "Test the 4 harmony voices" after starting to hear each one on its own.
